Biographical Spotlight

THE CHIEF ASSOCIATE OF LEGENDARY DIRECTOR KOTI

Tejanjali stands at the prestigious intersection of traditional Telugu artistry and modern cinematic execution. Guided under the direct mentorship of legendary music maestro Koti Garu, she has handled music production from conceptual inception through live recording, arrangement, and master releases.

Her artistic trajectory is marked by high versatility: composing, writing lyrics, executing complex choreography, anchoring grand musical events, and taking helm as Executive Producer. From intimate studio songwriting to headlining international stages at GAMA Dubai, her creative expression knows no boundaries.
